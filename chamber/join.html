NP Membership Benefits

Cost: Free for qualifying non-profit organizations

  • Listing in the Edo State Chamber directory
  • Access to monthly community networking meetings
  • Community partnership announcements

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: ₦50,000 / year

  • Standard business directory listing
  • Invitations to chamber mixers and trade forums
  • Monthly chamber newsletter updates

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: ₦120,000 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Discounts on business workshops and training sessions
  • Reduced tickets for annual agribusiness and trade summits

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: ₦250,000 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Featured spotlight rotation on the chamber home page
  • Priority sponsorship and seating at major chamber events
